// ABOUT US
Built for India's vertical future.

Space Park Systems engineers and installs mechanical parking solutions purpose-built for Indian conditions — dense urban cores, mixed-use towers, hospital and institutional campuses, and high-rise residences.

From two-car stackers to 72-car automated towers, every system is delivered as a turnkey package: site study, structural design, fabrication, installation, commissioning and lifetime AMC.

We work with developers, architects, RWAs, hospitals, airports and government projects who need to park more cars in less concrete.

// HOW WE WORK
From site visit to handover.

A disciplined four-stage delivery — no surprises, no scope creep, no missed deadlines.

STEP 01

Site Study

We measure your footprint, basement clearance, ramp angles and column grids. You get a system shortlist within 48 hours.

STEP 02

Design

Structural drawings, hydraulic schematics, PLC I/O tables and bill of materials — stamped and shared before any fabrication starts.

STEP 03

Install

Pre-fabricated steel skids, hydraulic packs and motors arrive on site. Typical commissioning: 14–21 days.

STEP 04

Handover

Operator training, manuals, spares kit and a 1-year warranty. Optional AMC packages extend cover to the full system lifecycle.
